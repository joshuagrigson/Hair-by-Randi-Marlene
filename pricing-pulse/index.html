Where the area average comes from. Each service starts from a typical US price for that service, scaled by your state's price level (approximate BEA Regional Price Parities) and whether you're rural, a small city, a suburb or a big city. Those starting numbers are estimates, not a survey. Every competitor price you add under “Local prices I've seen” pulls the average toward what your area actually charges — the estimate counts as two data points, so three or four real prices outweigh it.
