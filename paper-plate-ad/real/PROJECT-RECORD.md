# Paper Plate ad 03: project record

Kept with the master, as the Leonardo manual asks (sections 13, 38, 43).

**Acceptance sentence.** A 34-second vertical ad that reads as phone footage of a real woman at home: she asks what's for dinner, takes one photo of a half-stocked pantry, two shelves become two plated meals, she has an "AH HAH" and then relief, later shares a cooking stream to Paper Plate and sits back; the app on the phone is the real app.

## Failed approaches
- flux stills + Kling v3: graded, smooth skin, dark vignette. Reads as AI.
- Removing a baked vignette with ffmpeg: blew the corners out.
- Asking for a closer camera in words: barely moved. Crop the wide still and have the edit model re-photograph it.
- First "remove the box" edit: no change. Naming it by look and position, with a new seed, worked.
- Full-page screenshot of the app: fixed toolbars repeat mid-page.
- Dissolves between shots: read as graphics. Hard cuts now.

**Remaining limitations**
- The cooking stream and the iOS share sheet are drawn.
- The recipe on the real app screens was seeded, not generated by the app.
- The shelf-to-plate change is magic by design.
- No voice or music; the sound version has not been listened to.
- Her "AH HAH" eyes are very wide for a few frames.

**Decision:** accept as the working master; repair the stream and share sheet when a real screen recording exists.
